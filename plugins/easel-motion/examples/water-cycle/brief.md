# Brief: Where rain comes from (Easel example)

- **Idea:** the water in the rain was in the sea first. The spark rides one drop up, the drop
  becomes a cloud, the cloud gives the water back.
- **Format:** morph chain (F2), 8 s, 120 BPM, 24 fps; 16:9 (main) and 9:16 from one piece.
- **Style:** cut-paper. Hero: the spark.
- **Beats:** 0-4 s the sea (sun warms it, drops rise, the big drop rises with the spark);
  4 s morph drop -> cloud; 4-8 s the sky (rain from 5.5 s, the river fills from 6.5 s).
- **Sound:** a low pad, rising blips for the drops, a swish into the morph, rain hiss and a warm
  chord for the payoff.
- **Claims:** evaporation and precipitation (general science; no figures on screen).
