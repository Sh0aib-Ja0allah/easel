# Playful rounded

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere
Friendly and bright: rounded shapes, a cheerful primary with two supporting colours, chunky type, hand-written notes.

## 2. Color
- Primary: #5B3FD6 (buttons, links, key shapes)
- Secondary: #FFB020 (highlights, stickers; not for text)
- Tertiary: #1FB58F (success marks, illustrations; not for text)
- Success text: #016E55 (success messages)
- Background: #FFFBF3 (page)
- Surface: #FFFFFF (cards)
- Text: #231942 (body)
- Muted text: #5D5878 (secondary)
- Contrast: body text on background and on surface meets WCAG AA (4.5:1)

## 3. Typography
- Display: Space Grotesk 700, 40-72px
- Body: Inter 400, 17px / 1.6
- Notes and annotations: Caveat 700, 24px+
- Scale: 1.25

## 4. Spacing & Grid
8px base; 12-column grid, 24px gutters; generous padding inside cards (24-32px).

## 5. Layout & Composition
Centred or gently asymmetric; shapes may overlap card edges; one illustration per section at most.

## 6. Components
Pill buttons (999px radius) with a 2px darker bottom edge; cards 20px radius with a soft 0 8px 24px shadow tinted with the primary; badges as stickers.

## 7. Motion & Interaction
Springs allowed (a small overshoot of 4-8%) for entrances and presses; 200-350ms.

## 8. Voice & Brand
Warm, encouraging, a little funny; second person; short.

## 9. Anti-patterns
- Thin grey text
- Sharp corners
- Corporate stock photography
- More than three bright colours on one screen
