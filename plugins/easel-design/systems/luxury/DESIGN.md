# Restrained luxury

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere
Quiet and expensive: high-contrast serif display, lots of space, a deep ground, one metallic-feeling accent used only as a line.

## 2. Color
- Primary: #14110F (text, deep grounds)
- Accent: #B08D57 (hairlines, small marks only)
- Background: #F7F4EF (page)
- Surface: #EFEAE2 (panels)
- Text: #14110F (body)
- Muted text: #5F5850 (captions)
- Contrast: body text on background and on surface meets WCAG AA (4.5:1)

## 3. Typography
- Display: Newsreader 400, large (56-120px), tracking -0.01em
- Body: Inter 400, 15px / 1.7
- Labels: Inter 500, 11px, uppercase, 0.16em tracking
- Scale: 1.414

## 4. Spacing & Grid
8px base; wide outer margins (120px on desktop); a 12-column grid used sparsely.

## 5. Layout & Composition
Centred or strictly symmetrical; one image or one line of type per view; space is the luxury.

## 6. Components
Text links with a thin accent underline; buttons outlined, square corners, small caps label; no shadows.

## 7. Motion & Interaction
Slow and smooth: 500-800ms ease-in-out fades; nothing slides in fast.

## 8. Voice & Brand
Few words, chosen carefully; no prices in headlines; no exclamation marks.

## 9. Anti-patterns
- Gold gradients
- Busy layouts
- Bold sans-serif shouting
- Discount badges
