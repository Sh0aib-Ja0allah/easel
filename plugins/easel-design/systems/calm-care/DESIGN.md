# Calm care

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere
Reassuring and accessible: soft green-blue tones, large readable type, high contrast, nothing that rushes the reader.

## 2. Color
- Primary: #20706B (buttons, links)
- Secondary: #5E8FD0 (information: icons, fills and borders; not for text)
- Secondary text: #2F5E9B (information text and links in the secondary hue)
- Background: #F7FAF9 (page)
- Surface: #FFFFFF (cards)
- Text: #16302E (body)
- Muted text: #4C6260 (secondary)
- Warning: #9A5B00
- Error: #B42318
- Contrast: body text on background and on surface meets WCAG AA (4.5:1)

## 3. Typography
- Display: Space Grotesk 600, 32-48px
- Body: Inter 400, 18px / 1.65 (never below 16px)
- Scale: 1.2

## 4. Spacing & Grid
8px base; single column on phones, 8-column grid on desktop with 32px gutters; 48px minimum touch targets.

## 5. Layout & Composition
One task per screen; clear headings; steps numbered; generous spacing.

## 6. Components
Buttons 10px radius, 48px tall, full-width on phones; form fields with visible labels above, never placeholder-only; error text with an icon and a fix.

## 7. Motion & Interaction
Gentle 200-300ms fades; no parallax; reduced motion honoured fully.

## 8. Voice & Brand
Warm, plain language at a reading age of about 12; no jargon; say what happens next.

## 9. Anti-patterns
- Red for anything but errors
- Light grey text
- Dense tables on mobile
- Countdown timers or urgency
