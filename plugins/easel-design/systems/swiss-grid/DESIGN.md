# International grid

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere
Objective and structured: a strict modular grid, a grotesque sans in a few weights, flush-left ragged-right text, primary red as the only colour.

## 2. Color
- Primary: #E3001B (one shape or rule per layout)
- Background: #FFFFFF (page)
- Surface: #F0F0F0 (blocks)
- Text: #111111 (everything)
- Muted text: #555555 (captions)
- Contrast: body text on background and on surface meets WCAG AA (4.5:1)

## 3. Typography
- Display: Inter 700, 64-140px, tight leading (0.95)
- Body: Inter 400, 16px / 1.45
- Captions: Inter 500, 12px
- Scale: modular from the grid unit, not a ratio

## 4. Spacing & Grid
A modular grid of 6 columns and 8 rows on posters, 12 columns on screens; baseline grid of 8px; everything snaps to it.

## 5. Layout & Composition
Asymmetric, flush left, ragged right; large type as structure; photos cropped to grid modules.

## 6. Components
No decoration: rules and blocks only; buttons are text with an arrow or a solid red block; no radius.

## 7. Motion & Interaction
Straight cuts or quick slides along the grid (200ms linear).

## 8. Voice & Brand
Objective, factual, short.

## 9. Anti-patterns
- Centred text
- Script fonts
- Ornament
- More than one colour besides black
