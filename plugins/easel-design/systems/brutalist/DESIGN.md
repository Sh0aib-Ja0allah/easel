# Raw grid

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere
Raw and direct: visible structure, monospace, hard black borders, default-looking links on purpose, one acid accent.

## 2. Color
- Primary: #1A1A1A (borders, text, buttons)
- Accent: #D4FF3A (highlights, hover, one block per page: a fill behind black text; not for text)
- Background: #F2F2EE (page)
- Surface: #FFFFFF (boxes)
- Text: #1A1A1A (everything)
- Muted text: #4A4A44 (notes)
- Contrast: body text on background and on surface meets WCAG AA (4.5:1)

## 3. Typography
- Display: Space Grotesk 700, very large (72-160px), tight
- Body: JetBrains Mono 400, 15px / 1.55
- Labels: JetBrains Mono 700, uppercase
- Scale: 1.5 (big jumps, few steps)

## 4. Spacing & Grid
8px base; a visible 12-column grid (show the lines); 2px black borders on every box; no gutters between boxes, borders touch.

## 5. Layout & Composition
Boxes stacked on a grid; content left-aligned; the structure is the decoration; one oversized element per page.

## 6. Components
Buttons are bordered boxes that invert on hover; no radius anywhere; no shadows; tables with full borders.

## 7. Motion & Interaction
Instant or step changes (no easing); a hard cut is the transition.

## 8. Voice & Brand
Short, blunt, a little dry. Lowercase allowed.

## 9. Anti-patterns
- Soft shadows and rounded corners
- Gradients
- Decorative illustrations
- Subtle grey-on-grey text
