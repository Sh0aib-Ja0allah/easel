# Editorial

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere
A long-read publication: serif headlines with real contrast in scale, narrow columns, ink on warm paper, one red accent used sparingly.

## 2. Color
- Primary: #B3261E (rules, kickers, one highlight per spread)
- Background: #FBF8F2 (paper)
- Surface: #F2EDE3 (pull quotes, sidebars)
- Text: #1B1A17 (body and headlines)
- Muted text: #5E5A52 (bylines, captions)
- Border: #D9D1C3 (hairline rules)
- Contrast: body text on background and on surface meets WCAG AA (4.5:1)

## 3. Typography
- Display: Newsreader 600, large (56-96px), tight leading (1.05)
- Body: Newsreader 400, 19px / 1.6, 60-72 characters per line
- Labels and kickers: Inter 600, 12px, uppercase, 0.08em tracking
- Scale: 1.333 with a jump for the headline

## 4. Spacing & Grid
8px base unit; 6-column text grid inside a 12-column page; 64px outer margins on desktop; hairline rules between sections.

## 5. Layout & Composition
Asymmetric: headline across, text in a narrow column, images break the grid on purpose; drop caps allowed at the start of a story.

## 6. Components
Links underlined in the accent; pull quotes in display serif at 1.5x body; captions under images, never over them; no rounded corners.

## 7. Motion & Interaction
Almost none: fades of 200ms; never animate text while it is being read.

## 8. Voice & Brand
Measured and precise; headlines are statements, not questions; no exclamation marks.

## 9. Anti-patterns
- Sans-serif headlines in a news story
- Centred body text
- Coloured backgrounds behind long text
- Stock photos of people pointing at screens
