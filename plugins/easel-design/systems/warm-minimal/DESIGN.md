# Paper and ink

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere
Quiet and warm: off-white paper, deep ink blue, a single terracotta accent, lots of air, small type used with care.

## 2. Color
- Primary: #1D3557 (text and key shapes)
- Accent: #C8553D (one accent per layout: shapes and rules; not for text, nor as a fill behind white text)
- Accent text: #A1311A (accent words and links; the fill behind white button text)
- Background: #F4EFE6 (paper)
- Surface: #EBE4D8 (panels)
- Text: #1D3557 (body)
- Muted text: #5A6478 (captions)
- Contrast: body text on background and on surface meets WCAG AA (4.5:1)

## 3. Typography
- Display: Space Grotesk 700, 40-64px
- Body: Newsreader 400, 18px / 1.55
- UI: Inter 400, 14px
- Scale: 1.25

## 4. Spacing & Grid
8px base unit; 12-column grid with 24px gutters; 80px outer margins on desktop.

## 5. Layout & Composition
Asymmetric and left-aligned; one focal point per frame; empty space is part of the layout.

## 6. Components
Cards with 12px radius and no shadow, separated by space rather than borders; buttons solid ink with paper text.

## 7. Motion & Interaction
Ease-out 250-400ms; nothing bounces; one movement at a time.

## 8. Voice & Brand
Plain, confident, short sentences.

## 9. Anti-patterns
- Blue-purple gradients and glassmorphism
- Pure white backgrounds
- More than two typefaces
- Emoji
