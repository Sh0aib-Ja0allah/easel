How it works

From the first call to walking without a stick, you always know what happens next.

  1. Step 1

    A free 20-minute call

    We ask about your operation, your home and what you want to get back to doing.

  2. Step 2

    Your plan, by post and online

    A printed exercise plan and a resistance band arrive within two working days.

  3. Step 3

    Sessions three times a week

    Your physio watches each movement on video and adjusts the plan as you get stronger.

What every session includes

Thirty to forty-five minutes, at a time that suits you, on a phone, tablet or laptop.

Exercises checked live

Your physio sees how you move and corrects it there and then, so you never practise a habit that slows healing.

Progress you can see

Knee bend, walking distance and pain scores are measured each week and shared with you and, if you wish, your surgeon.

Help between sessions

Send a message about swelling or a sore step on the stairs and your physio replies the same working day.

Talk to a physio before you decide.

The first call is free and there is nothing to sign. If home sessions are not right for you, we will tell you what is.
