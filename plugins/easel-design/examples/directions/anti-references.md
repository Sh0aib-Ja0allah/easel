# Anti-references: hero for Grepwell (fictional local log search)

Brief: a hero for Grepwell, a single binary that searches a week of logs on your own laptop, no
server. System: dark-tech (`systems/dark-tech/DESIGN.md`) fixes the colours and faces; the
directions vary composition, type scale, density and copy tone.

The generic default for this brief, which no direction may keep more than two of:

1. Centred headline, one-line subhead, two buttons side by side under it.
2. A blue-to-purple gradient glow behind the headline.
3. A floating laptop or terminal-window screenshot with a drop shadow.
4. Three feature cards in a row (icon, title, two lines) below the fold line.
5. A "trusted by" strip of grey company logos.
6. "Supercharge your observability" style copy: a verb of hype plus a category noun.
7. Neon glow on every edge and button.

| Direction | File | Convention it breaks | Defaults kept |
|---|---|---|---|
| The query is the headline | `a-query-headline.html` | No marketing headline and no screenshot: the command you would type is the headline. | none |
| Log wall | `b-log-wall.html` | No empty hero space: the page is the log itself, and the pitch is one matching line in it. | none |
| Zero servers | `c-zero-servers.html` | Leads with one huge number set into the margin instead of a promise, with no cards. | 1 (two buttons, left-aligned) |
