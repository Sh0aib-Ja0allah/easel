October to February

Three changes kept the shop open all winter.

  • Night bakingFirst loaves now leave the oven at 6:30, not 8:00, so the early commuters stay.
  • A sourdough subscription120 households pay monthly and collect on Tuesdays and Fridays.
  • Apprentices on SaturdaysTwo apprentices finished their first year and now run the weekend bake.
