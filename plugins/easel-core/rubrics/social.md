# Social rubric

Shared scale and rules: [README](README.md). Review every exported size side by side
(`contact_sheet`). Score each dimension 1-10.

| Dimension | What a 7 looks like |
|---|---|
| **Thumb-stop** | At feed size (about 360 px wide) one element stops the scroll |
| **Readability on a phone** | Headline readable at feed size; at most about 7 words on a thumbnail |
| **Safe zones** | Nothing important under platform UI (profile, buttons, captions) in any size |
| **Format coverage** | Every requested size exists and is recomposed, not just cropped |
| **Brand and voice** | On-brand colours and type; copy in the brand voice |
| **Technical** | Exact pixel sizes; text contrast passes (`qa` contrast); video: captions on, loudness in range |
