# Example Studio brand

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere
Calm, warm and precise. Generous white space, paper textures, no gloss.

## 2. Color
- Primary: #1D3557 (ink blue), text and key shapes
- Accent: #E76F51 (terracotta), one accent per layout
- Background: #F4EFE6 (paper)
- Neutral: #6B7280
- Contrast: body text on background must meet WCAG AA

## 3. Typography
- Display: Space Grotesk 700
- Body: Newsreader 400, 18px / 1.5
- UI and data: Inter, JetBrains Mono for numbers

## 4. Spacing & Grid
8px base unit; 12-column grid with 24px gutters; 64px outer margins on 1920 wide.

## 5. Layout & Composition
Asymmetric, left-aligned; one focal point per frame.

## 6. Components
Cards with 12px radius, no shadows; buttons are solid primary with paper text.

## 7. Motion & Interaction
Ease-out 250-400ms; nothing bounces; one movement at a time.

## 8. Voice & Brand
Plain, confident, short sentences. No exclamation marks.

## 9. Anti-patterns
- Blue-purple gradients and glassmorphism
- Stock-photo people, emoji, more than two typefaces

## Logo
Mark: ./brand/mark.svg; minimum size 24px; clear space equals the mark's height / 4.
